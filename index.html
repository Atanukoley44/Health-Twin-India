About Health Twin India

Health Twin India

Health Twin India is building a connected digital healthcare ecosystem that brings together prescription support, medicine availability and price comparison, and digital health records—all in one place, making healthcare simpler and more accessible.


Our Vision

To build a trusted Digital Health ecosystem where individuals can securely manage their health information & can find medicines nearby their location.

Our Mission

To empower individuals through a connected digital healthcare platform that brings more access to medicines & managing prescription & health reports in one platform.

Our Purpose

Better Access. Better Care. Better Health for All.

Important Notes for Healthcare Service Requests

  • Provide only the minimum information required for your selected service.
  • Select the appropriate service: Prescription Clarification, Find Your Medicine, or Digital Health Records.
  • Upload prescriptions or medical documents only when relevant to your request.
  • Do not upload emergency medical requests. Seek immediate medical assistance when needed.
  • Your health information must be handled in accordance with the applicable privacy policy, consent requirements, and data protection obligations.
  • Prescription-related decisions and medication changes must be guided by a qualified healthcare professional.
  • Medicine availability, prices, and service responses are subject to verification and confirmation.
