About Health Twin India

Health Twin India

Health Twin India is building a connected digital healthcare ecosystem that brings together prescriptions, medicines, diagnostic services, healthcare providers, and personal health information — making healthcare more accessible, transparent, and patient-centric.


Our Vision

To build a trusted Digital Health ecosystem where individuals can securely manage their health information & can find medicines nearby their location.

Our Mission

To empower individuals through a connected digital healthcare platform that brings more access to medicines & managing prescription & health reports in one platform.

Our Purpose

Better Access. Better Care. Better Health for All.

Important Notes (For Prescription Upload)

  • Mention Privacy Policy and Terms clearly.
  • Do not store prescription directly in public server/storage.
  • Use secure backend when dynamic version is ready.
  • Ensure compliance with DPDP Act, 2023 and ABDM guidelines.
